What is a Safe Space?

A safe space is an environment where Golden Valley School students can feel secure, respected, and free from judgment or harm, allowing them to express themselves openly.

Three signs of a Safe Space:

1. Active Listening

People listen to your concerns without interrupting or judging you.

2. Confidentiality

What you share is kept private and respected to protect your privacy and trust.

3. Zero Tolerance for Bullying

Harassment or bullying is never accepted, and swift support is always available.
